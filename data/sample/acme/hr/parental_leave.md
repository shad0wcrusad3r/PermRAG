# Parental Leave Policy

Acme Corp offers **16 weeks of paid parental leave** to all employees who become parents through birth, adoption, or foster care placement.

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed at least 6 months of service are eligible.

## How to Apply

Submit a leave request through the HR portal at least 30 days before the expected start date.

## Benefits During Leave

- Full salary continuation
- Healthcare benefits maintained
- Retirement contributions continue
