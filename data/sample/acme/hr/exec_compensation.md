# Executive Compensation 2026

**CONFIDENTIAL - HR Admin Only**

This document contains sensitive compensation information for Acme Corp executive leadership.

## C-Suite Base Salaries

- CEO: $485,000
- CFO: $412,000
- CTO: $395,000
- COO: $405,000

## Bonus Structure

Executive bonuses are calculated as a percentage of base salary and are tied to company performance metrics.

Target bonuses range from 40% to 60% of base salary for executive roles.

## Equity Grants

All executives receive annual equity grants valued between $200,000 and $500,000.
