# Globex Employee Benefits 2026

At Globex, we provide comprehensive benefits to support our employees and their families.

## Health Insurance

- Medical, dental, and vision coverage
- Company pays 80% of premiums for employee coverage
- Family coverage available with employee contribution

## Parental Leave

Globex offers **12 weeks of paid parental leave** for all full-time employees.

## Retirement

- 401(k) plan with 5% company match
- Immediate vesting
- Financial planning services available

## Paid Time Off

- 15 days vacation (accrues over the year)
- 10 sick days
- 11 company holidays
