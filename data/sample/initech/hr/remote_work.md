# Remote Work Policy

Initech supports flexible work arrangements to help employees maintain work-life balance.

## Eligibility

All employees who have completed their probationary period (90 days) are eligible for remote work.

## Guidelines

- Remote work available up to 3 days per week
- Must be available during core hours (10am-3pm local time)
- Maintain responsiveness on Slack and email
- Attend all scheduled team meetings

## Equipment

Initech provides:
- Laptop computer
- Monitor (upon request)
- Headset for video calls

## Productivity Expectations

Remote work is a privilege. Employees must maintain the same productivity standards whether working remotely or in the office.

Managers may require in-office presence for specific projects or meetings.
