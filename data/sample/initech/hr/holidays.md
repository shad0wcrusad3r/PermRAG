# Company Holidays 2026

All Initech offices will be closed on the following holidays:

## US Holidays

- January 1: New Year's Day
- January 20: Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- May 26: Memorial Day
- July 4: Independence Day (Friday)
- September 1: Labor Day
- November 27-28: Thanksgiving (Thursday and Friday)
- December 25: Christmas Day

## Floating Holidays

Each employee receives 2 floating holiday days to use at their discretion. These must be scheduled in advance with manager approval.

## Holiday Pay

All full-time employees receive regular pay for company holidays. Part-time employees receive holiday pay proportional to their scheduled hours.
