# Layoff Plan - January 2026

**HIGHLY CONFIDENTIAL - HR Admin and Executive Team Only**

Due to market conditions, Initech will conduct a workforce reduction affecting approximately 15% of staff.

## Timeline

- January 15: Finalize list of affected positions
- January 22: Notify affected employees
- January 29: Last day for affected employees
- February 1: Severance payments begin

## Affected Departments

- Engineering: 8 positions
- Sales: 12 positions  
- Marketing: 5 positions
- Operations: 3 positions

Total: 28 positions

## Severance Package

- 2 weeks pay per year of service (minimum 4 weeks)
- Extended healthcare coverage for 3 months
- Outplacement services provided

## Communication Plan

This information must remain strictly confidential until official announcements. Premature disclosure could cause unnecessary anxiety and potential legal issues.

Only the CEO, CFO, and VP of HR have access to the final list of names.
